Rejected root experiments
-------------------------
* Joint insertion DP enumerates all moving-paper subsets and interleavings with
  the retained children within each selected owner. It minimizes the existing
  sampled mandatory-removal union, then length inversions. Ten seeds, cap 5:
  13264.9; cap 7: 13265.6. No group had a strict primary-objective improvement
  over the original greedy method. Changed outputs were tie changes. CPU grew.
  See round7_joint_insert.h and *_results.json.
* Owner control variate: sampled full-goal union minus sampled private-owner
  union plus the exact uniform-product owner probability. Each product's
  longest full-prefix-matching owner defines a disjoint event. Beta 1 gave
  13247.2 on 10 seeds, but 13337.26 on 100, only 0.47 better than reference.
  Beta .5/2/4 on ten seeds gave 13254.3/13482.7/13503.5. Rejected. This is a
  variance-control heuristic: actual FIFO samples are correlated with the state,
  so there is no claim of strict unbiasedness for the online estimator.
* Smooth allocations of the 127 additional papers over lengths, followed by
  exact refit, did not improve the reference. Five profiles at R4, three seeds:
  uniform from 33:14205.67; linear from25:13741; quadratic from17:13516.67;
  uniform from41:13983; linear from33:13395.67. Lower intrinsic cover counts
  alone did not predict fewer actual operations.
* Persistent goal price: exact current moves + lambda * visible-record count.
  The reconstructed goal's actual legal distance is recomputed independently
  before emitting operations. Three-seed scores for lambda .1/.25/.5/1:
  13579.33/13606.67/13806.33/14369. Negative lambda -.1/-.25/-.5/-1/-2:
  13274.33/13422.67/18093.33/24166.33/24453.33. Rejected.
* Adjacent column swap preserves physical colors on all papers except the
  boundary level, whose dictionary is refitted. Best exact snapshot move sum
  determines an accepted swap. Four passes at one/eight snapshots per product:
  three-seed means 13449.67/13343.67; one pass at eight snapshots:13312.67.
  Rejected; this direction also overlaps the older round5 joint experiment.
* Initial intrinsic refit 8 and16 passes produces the same three-seed outputs
  as4 passes (already converged). Two passes worsens mean to13438.

Equivalent speedups under validation
------------------------------------
* PrefixDP prepares three 64-bit position masks per length rather than filling
  65x193 integer prefix counts. An edge counts long papers in [h,q), and tests
  whether that range contains any paper whose length lies in (j,l).
  PREFIX_MASK_CHECK checks the old formula. One million random edge checks pass.
  Prefix range masks depend only on K and are initialized once per DP object.
* Terminal insertion specializes to the checked partition <L, =L, >L.
  Same-length sampled goals are disjoint. Outside the =L block, crossing a
  shorter or longer paper cannot decrease the union objective and adds an
  inversion. Within the block, the incremental objective is freq[new]-freq[x].
  Other states or overlapping samples use the original general implementation.
  The helper alone matches all stdout bytes on 100 fixed-R8 seeds. Combined
  helpers match complete stdout for fixed R8/R32 on five seeds each. Random
  permutations, selected goals and access masks exercise 4200 calls, including
  3151 general fallbacks, with exact reference equality.
* Sequential fixed-R32 five-seed CPU: reference roughly1.55..1.94 seconds,
  combined roughly1.41..1.76 seconds. Do not treat concurrent timing as a
  hardware-independent speed guarantee. Optional popcnt multiversioning was
  tested but is not needed for the selected portable implementation.
* Old adaptive capture factor5 becomes too optimistic after capture speeds up:
  first100-case adaptive trial mean13372.39, only25 complete exact-refit runs.
  Sampling calibration is being adjusted before installing any candidate.
