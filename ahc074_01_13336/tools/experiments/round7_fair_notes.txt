Round 7: product cost disparity and weighted representative initialization
=======================================================================

Scope and baseline
------------------
All changes are experiment-only; main.cpp, a.out, and pahcer outputs were not
modified. Baseline main SHA-256 was
9c78b086622882223782412512fa1ab4b3d2b0c538e818f75be0aa5f4cbba7f4.
All reported experiments use fixed repetitions and diagnostic deadlines of
100 CPU seconds, with at most two official tester workers. These timings do
not certify a production two-second setting. Paper lengths remain unchanged:
cutoff 53, eight extras at upper levels and four below.

Product measurements
--------------------
round7_fair_metrics.h uses only public A and internal requests. After 128
warmup requests it simulates 1024 balanced internal requests; at 32 sampled
states it evaluates the exact shortest distance to all products, excluding
the previous product. It also records each product's longest completely
matching prefix paper and the selected goal records.

On seeds 0, 1, 2, the correlation between longest matching prefix length and
expected next-request cost was -0.96249, -0.96025, -0.96334. The 106 products
with no matching prefix of length >=43 cost 19.86, 19.88, 20.14 operations on
average. Products with a length 61..64 matching prefix cost about 6.0.

Seed 0 groups (length range / products / expected moves / selected records):
  0..42  / 106 / 19.8608 / 31.1533
  43..52 /  41 /  9.5475 / 12.9085
  53..56 /  36 /  9.1781 /  8.3333
  57..60 /  36 /  7.6650 /  5.9653
  61..64 /  37 /  6.0116 /  2.5541

For every product in these three seeds, the longest fully matching prefix
paper was selected on all four internal visits. This is empirical evidence,
not a proof that this paper is always selected. It suggests a stable owner
component for future access estimation, distinct from a stale complete goal.

Experiments
-----------
The weighted objective uses weight[v] = clamp(round(32 * (mean_cost[v] /
global_mean)^alpha), 8, 128). Exact DP distances remain unweighted uint8;
only trie benefits and color-change acceptance totals are weighted.
Reachability-critical gains are weighted too. Initial trie DP accumulators
were widened to int64 to prevent overflow when applying weights to 1e6
critical benefits. An alpha=1e-9 control produces all weights 32 and exactly
matches all three baseline scores, checking the weighting plumbing.

All means below are official tester scores with E=0, seeds starting at 0.

Single weighted exact R8, three seeds:
  baseline alpha 0: 13276.67
  alpha +0.5:       13880.00
  alpha +1:         13985.00
  alpha +2:         14257.00
  alpha -0.5:       13800.33
  alpha -1:         13768.67

Weighted escape followed by uniform refit on freshly resimulated states:
  alpha .5, R4 then uniform R4: 13227.33 on 3 seeds,
                               13243.10 on 10 seeds,
                               13398.40 on 30 seeds.
  Baseline R8 on same 30 seeds: 13308.77. Therefore rejected (+89.63).
  Uniform R4 then uniform R4 control: 13382.67 on 3 seeds.
  alpha .25, R4 then uniform R4: 13266.00 on 3 seeds.
  alpha .75, R4 then uniform R4: 13248.00 on 3 seeds.
  alpha .5, R8 then uniform R8: 13339.33 on 3 seeds.

Large initialization mutation: learn the ordinary dictionary, measure
product costs using four internally sampled repetitions without refitting,
then rebuild the universal backbone and 127 extra representative patterns.
Subsequent intrinsic and exact phases may use uniform weights again.

Weight only the representative allocation (alpha 1), then uniform intrinsic
four-pass refit and uniform exact refit:
  R8:  13226.33 on 3 seeds, 13370.03 on 30 seeds.
       Same-30 baseline R8 = 13308.77; delta +61.27.
  R32: 13320.90 on 30 seeds.
       Same-30 baseline R32 = 13299.80; delta +21.10.
  R8 mean/max CPU .6679/.7099 seconds; R32 2.0015/2.2017 seconds,
  under two diagnostic workers. No production guard was evaluated.

Other initialization probes (3 seeds, R8):
  allocation only, alpha .5: 13344.33
  intrinsic only, alpha .5:  13400.33
  both stages, alpha .5:     13355.33
  both stages, alpha 1:      13712.67

Conclusion
----------
The large product cost disparity is real, but directly emphasizing expensive
products does not improve the uniform average. Promising three/ten-seed
effects disappeared on 30 seeds, so no candidate was promoted to 100 seeds
or to main.cpp. The data support testing structure or access-policy changes
instead of repeating scalar product fairness weights.
